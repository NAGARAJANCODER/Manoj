About Us

The Tamil Nadu Motorcyclists Welfare Association brings riders together around a shared love of the open road and a responsibility to one another.

We create welcoming opportunities to ride, explore new places and build lasting friendships. From the first route briefing to the final miles home, we encourage thoughtful planning, safe riding and respect for the communities we visit.

Whether you are joining your first group ride or have travelled for years, TNMWA is a place to connect with fellow riders, share knowledge and make every journey count.

Why we ride

Our Mission

Our mission is to make motorcycling more connected, responsible and rewarding for riders across Tamil Nadu.

We bring people together through well-planned rides, practical road-safety awareness and a supportive community where riders look out for one another.

Every ride is a chance to learn, lend a hand and help make the road a better place for everyone.

Many roads, one community

Our Journey

Every journey starts with people who are ready to ride together. TNMWA brings those riders into one community built on shared experiences and mutual support.

Group rides connect us with the landscapes, towns and people of Tamil Nadu. Careful route planning, clear ride briefings and respect for local communities help each trip feel welcoming and well organised.

As more riders join, our community keeps moving forward: exploring new routes, sharing what we learn and making room for the next person to come along.
