Why we ride

Our Mission

TNMWA brings motorcyclists together to ride with honor, encourage responsible riding and look out for one another.

Through organized group rides, shared road-safety awareness and community support, we help every rider feel welcome on the road.

Every journey is a chance to build friendships, confidence and respect for the places we travel.

Many roads, one community

Our Journey

A shared love of motorcycling brings our community together. Each ride gives us a new road to discover and new people to meet.

From planning routes and sharing ride briefings to supporting one another along the way, we make group riding a welcoming experience.

Today, TNMWA connects over 1,000 riders, has organised more than 30 rides and explored 15+ destinations together.
